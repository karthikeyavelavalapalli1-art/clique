EEE Department Unity

Exclusive to EEE Branch

CLIQUE is strictly for Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE). We bring 1st-year EEE juniors and 2nd-year EEE seniors into one informal, welcoming room every Saturday.

4-Stage Quiz Tournament

Stage 1 MCQs (Basic Engg, Current Affairs & G.K) ➔ Stage 2 Quiz ➔ Stage 3 Top 4 Teams ➔ Stage 4 Grand Finale Top 2 Teams!

Faculty Certificate Awarding

Winners and Runners-up are officially awarded certificates of excellence presented directly in the presence of EEE Faculty!

Senior-Junior EEE Unity

Organized by 2nd-year EEE seniors for mandatory participation of 1st-year EEE juniors to build lifelong department bonds.

Tournament Roadmap

The 4 Competition Stages

The Saturday event progresses through 4 competitive stages leading up to the grand championship ceremony.

Stage 1
Elimination Round

MCQs: Basic Engg, Current Affairs & G.K

Multiple Choice Question test testing foundational Basic Engineering concepts, General Knowledge, and Current Affairs to filter top qualifying participants.

Stage 2
Interactive Quiz

Department Quiz Round

Fast-paced interactive buzzer quiz for qualifying teams, testing problem solving, engineering logic, and quick thinking.

Stage 3
Semi-Finals

Top 4 Teams Battle

Head-to-head semi-final quiz round between the 4 highest-scoring qualified teams competing for the 2 final championship spots.

Stage 4
Grand Finale & Awards

Top 2 Teams Finale & Faculty Award Ceremony

Championship finale between the Top 2 teams! Winners and Runners-up receive official certificates presented in the presence of EEE Faculty.

Stage 1 is an MCQ test on Basic Engineering, Current Affairs & G.K. Stage 2 is an interactive Quiz. Stage 3 is a semi-final showdown between the Top 4 teams, and Stage 4 is the Grand Finale between the Top 2 teams!

The Winners and Runners-up receive official certificates presented directly in the presence of EEE department faculty members during the closing ceremony!

Exclusively 1st-year EEE juniors and 2nd-year EEE senior mentors. 3rd and 4th year students and other engineering branches are not involved.

CLIQUE is held every Saturday in the EEE Department Classroom during the Morning Session (9:30 AM) or Afternoon Session (2:00 PM).

If you are a 2nd-year EEE student, select '2nd Year Senior Mentor' when completing the RSVP form. You will help organize the 4 quiz stages, mentor 1st years, and present certificates alongside faculty!
